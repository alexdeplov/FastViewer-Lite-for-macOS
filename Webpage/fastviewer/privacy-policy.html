Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 21, 2025

FastViewer ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our macOS application.

Summary: FastViewer does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All data remains on your device and is never shared with us or third parties.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

FastViewer is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports (unless you explicitly send them)
  • Image file contents or metadata
  • File paths or directory structures
  • Any data that leaves your device

2. Local Data Storage

FastViewer stores the following information locally on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults:

  • Application Preferences: Your display settings (file info visibility, file name display location, format support preferences)
  • Window Settings: Window size preferences and auto-resize settings
  • File Association Status: Whether default file associations have been set (first launch flag)

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted or shared. You can reset all preferences by deleting the app's UserDefaults or uninstalling the application.

3. Image Caching

FastViewer uses local image caching to improve performance. Cached images are stored temporarily on your device's storage and are:

  • Stored locally on your Mac
  • Never transmitted over the network
  • Automatically managed by the system
  • Cleared when you delete the app

4. File Access

FastViewer accesses image files only when you:

  • Open files directly through the app
  • Open files via "Open with" from Finder
  • Navigate through images in a folder

All file access is performed locally on your device. We use macOS security-scoped resources to respect sandboxing restrictions. No file contents or paths are ever transmitted or stored outside your device.

5. Network Access

FastViewer does not require internet connectivity and does not make any network requests. The application operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

FastViewer does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data collection services. The app uses only native macOS frameworks provided by Apple.

7. Default File Associations

On first launch, FastViewer may attempt to set itself as the default handler for image files (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF). This is a local system preference change and does not involve transmitting any data. If the app is sandboxed (as required for App Store distribution), you may need to manually set these associations in System Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

FastViewer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since we do not collect any data, the app is safe for users of all ages.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Your Rights

Since FastViewer does not collect or store your personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. All application data is stored locally on your device, and you have full control over it. You can:

  • Reset all preferences through the app's settings
  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Clear cached images through macOS storage management
